Day by Day

The unfolding

A sample flow. Every journey is fully personalised, so days, lodges and pacing are shaped around you.

  1. Met on arrival and transferred privately to your hotel. An evening to rest, dine and reset before the journey north.

  2. A short internal flight and private transfer to your lodge. Settle in, then a first glimpse of the spray on the horizon at sunset.

  3. Guided walking tour of the falls along the rainforest path, Zimbabwe or Zambia side. Afternoon at leisure; sunset cruise on the Zambezi.

  4. Optional helicopter flight or zip‑line over the gorge, or a slower morning by the pool. Dinner cruise or lodge dining, as you prefer.

  5. Fly to the lowveld and transfer into the private reserve. Arrive in time for your first afternoon game drive and sundowners in the bush.

  6. Dawn and dusk drives with your guide and tracker, bush walks where permitted, long lunches, and afternoons of stillness between them. Shabbat, when it falls, is arranged around you.

  7. One more morning in the bush, a final evening drive, and a farewell dinner under the stars.

  8. Transfer and internal flight to Johannesburg to connect with your international departure.
